# **Tari Network Terminology**

Below is a list of terms and their definitions that are used throughout the Tari code and documentation. Let’s use this glossary to disambiguate ideas and work towards a [**ubiquitous language**](https://blog.carbonfive.com/2016/10/04/ubiquitous-language-the-joy-of-naming/) for this project.

## **Archive node**

This is a full history [**base node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-node "A full Tari node running on the base layer, validating and propagating Tari coin transactions
and blocks"). It will keep a complete history of every transaction ever received, and it will not implement pruning.

## **AssetCollateral**

The amount of tari coin that a [**Validator Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#validator-node "A second-layer node that manages and validates digital asset state transitions") must put up on the [**base layer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-layer "The Tari layer handling payments and secured by proof of work") to become part of an asset [**committee**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#committee "A group of validator nodes that are responsible for managing a specific Digital Asset").

## **Asset Issuer**

An entity that creates digital assets on the Tari DAN. The Asset Issuer will specify the parameters of the contract template that defines the rules that govern the asset and the number and nature of its constituent tokens on issuance. The Asset Issuer will, generally, be the initial owner of the tokens.

## **Bad Actor**

A participant that acts maliciously or negligently to the detriment of the network or another participant.

## **Base layer**

The Tari Base layer is a merge-mined [**blockchain**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#blockchain "The linked sequence of Tari blocks on the Tari base layer") secured by proof-of-work. The base layer is primarily responsible for the emission of new Tari, for securing and managing [**Tari coin**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#tari-coin "The base layer token") transfers.

## **Base Node**

A full Tari node running on the base layer. Its primary role is validating and propagating [**Tari coin**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#tari-coin "The base layer token") transactions and blocks to the rest of the network.

## **Block**

A collection of transactions and associated metadata recorded as a single entity in the Tari blockchain. The ordering of Tari transactions is set purely by the block height of the block they are recorded in.

## **Block Header**

A data structure that validates the information contained in a [**block**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#block "A collection of transactions and associated metadata recorded as a single entity in the Tari blockchain").

## **Block Body**

A data structure containing the transaction inputs, outputs, and kernels that make up the block.

## **Block reward**

The amount of Tari created by the coinbase transaction in every block. The block reward is set by the [**emission schedule**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#emission-schedule).

## **Blockchain**

A sequence of Tari [**block**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#block "A collection of transactions and associated metadata recorded as a single entity in the Tari blockchain")s. Each block contains a hash of the previous valid block. Thus the blocks form a chain with the property that changing anything in a block other than the head block requires rewriting the entire blockchain from that point on.

## **Blockchain state**

The complete state of the blockchain at a specific block height. This means a pruned **UTXO** set, a complete set of kernels and headers up to that block height from the genesis block.

## **BroadcastStrategy**

A strategy for propagating messages amongst nodes in a peer-to-peer network. Example implementations of `BroadcastStrategy` include the Gossip protocol and flood fill.

## **Chain Reorganization**

A chain reorganization occurs after a chain split occurs on the network, which commonly occurs due to network latency and connectivity issues. When a chain split occurs, one chain will have the higher accumulated proof-of-work; this chain is considered the best chain. Nodes on the poorer chain will need to rewind and resync their chains to the best chain. In this process, transactions in the mempool could become orphaned or invalid.

## **Checkpoint**

A hash of the state of a Digital Asset that is recorded on the base layer.

## **Coinbase transaction**

The first transaction in every Tari block yields a [**Block Reward**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#block-reward "The amount of Tari created in every block") according to the Tari [**emission Schedule**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#emission-schedule) and is awarded to the miner that performed the Proof of Work for the block.

## **Coinbase extra**

An arbitrary 64 bytes can be included with the coinbase utxo and can be used to store any data. This can be used to identify the pool that mined the block, for example or a Merkle root used when merge mining with Tari as the parent chain.

## **Committee**

A group of [**Validator Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#validator-node "A second-layer node that manages and validates digital asset state transitions")s that are responsible for managing the state of a specific [**Digital Asset**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset "Sets of Native digital tokens, both fungible and non-fungible that are created by
asset issuers on the Tari 2nd layer"). A committee is selected during asset issuance and can be updated at [**Checkpoint**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#checkpoint "A summary of the state of a Digital Asset that is recorded on the base layer")s.

## **CommitteeSelectionStrategy**

A strategy for an Asset Issuer to select candidates for the committee from the available registered Validator Nodes who responded to the nomination call for that asset.

## **ConsensusStrategy**

The approach that will be taken for a committee to reach consensus on the validity of instructions that are performed on a given Digital Asset.

## **Commitment**

A commitment is a cryptographic primitive that allows one to commit to a chosen value while keeping it hidden from others, with the ability to reveal the committed value later. Commitments are designed so that one cannot change the value or statement after they have committed to it.

## [**Commitment and Public Key Signature**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#commitment-and-public-key-signature)

A mathematical assertion of knowledge of the opening of a [**commitment**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#commitment) and the private key corresponding to a public key, and which is bound to a message to produce a signature. In the context of Tari protocols, it is used to construct a [**metadata signature**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#metadata-signature) and script signature for transactions.

Specifically, it is a Schnorr-type conjunction proof that uses the Fiat-Shamir technique for message binding.

## [**Communication Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#communication-node)

A Communication Node is either a Validator Node or Base Node that is part of the Tari communication network. It maintains the network and is responsible for forwarding and propagating joining requests, discovery requests and data messages on the communication network.

## [**Communication Client**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#communication-client)

A Communication Client is a Wallet or Asset Manager that makes use of the Tari communication network to send joining and discovery requests. A Communication Client does not maintain the communication network and is not responsible for forwarding or propagating any requests or data messages.

## [**Creator Nomination Mode**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#creator-nomination-mode)

An asset runs in creator nomination mode when _every_ validator node in a validator committee is a [**Trusted Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#trusted-node "A permissioned Validator Node nominated by an Asset Issuer") that was directly nominated by the [**Asset Issuer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#asset-issuer "An entity that creates digital assets on the Tari DAN").

## [**Current head**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#current-head)

The last [**block**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#block "A collection of transactions and associated metadata recorded as a single entity in the Tari blockchain") of the base layer that represents the latest valid block. This [**block**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#block "A collection of transactions and associated metadata recorded as a single entity in the Tari blockchain") must be from the longest proof-of-work chain to be the current head.

## [**Cut-Through**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#cut-through)

Cut-through is the process where outputs spent within a single block may be removed without breaking the standard [**MimbleWimble**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mimblewimble) validation rules. Simplistically, `Alice -> Bob -> Carol` may be “cut-through” to `Alice -> Carol`. Bob’s commitments may be removed.

On Tari, for reasons described in [**RFC-0201_TariScript**](https://rfc.tari.com/RFC-0201_TariScript.html#utxo-data-commitments), cut-through is prevented from ever happening.

## [**Digital asset**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset)

Digital assets (DAs) are the sets or collections of native digital tokens (both fungible and non-fungible) that are created by [**asset issuer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#asset-issuer "An entity that creates digital assets on the Tari DAN")s on the Tari 2nd layer. For example, a promoter might create a DA for a music concert event. The event is the digital asset, and the tickets for the event are digital asset [**tokens**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset-tokens "or just, "tokens". The tokens associated with a given digital asset. Tokens are created
by asset issuers").

## [**Digital Asset Network**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset-network)

The Tari second layer. All digital asset interactions are managed on the Tari Digital Assets Network (DAN). These interactions (defined in [**instruction**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#instructions "Second-layer network commands for managing digital asset state")s) are processed and validated by [**Validator Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#validator-node "A second-layer node that manages and validates digital asset state transitions")s.

## [**DigitalAssetTemplate**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digitalassettemplate)

A DigitalAssetTemplate is one of a set of contract types supported by the DAN. These contracts are non-Turing complete and consist of rigid rule sets with parameters that can be set by Asset Issuers.

## [**Digital asset tokens**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset-tokens)

Digital asset tokens (or often, just “tokens”) are the finite set of digital entities associated with a given digital asset. Depending on the DA created, tokens can represent tickets, in-game items, collectibles or loyalty points. They are bound to the [**digital asset**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset "Sets of Native digital tokens, both fungible and non-fungible that are created by
asset issuers on the Tari 2nd layer") that created them.

## [**Hashed Time Locked Contract**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#hashed-time-locked-contract)

A time-locked contract that only pays out after a certain criterion has been met or refunds the originator if a certain period has expired.

## [**Emission schedule**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#emission-schedule)

An explicit formula as a function of the block height, _h_, that determines the block reward for the _h_<sup>th</sup> block.

## [**Instructions**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#instructions)

Instructions are the [**digital asset network**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset-network "The Tari second layer. All digital asset interactions are managed here.") equivalent of [**transaction**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#transaction "Base layer tari coin transfers.")s. Instructions are issued by asset issuers and client applications and are relayed by the DAN to the [**validator node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#validator-node "A second-layer node that manages and validates digital asset state transitions")s that are managing the associated [**digital asset**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset "Sets of Native digital tokens, both fungible and non-fungible that are created by
asset issuers on the Tari 2nd layer").

## [**Mempool**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mempool)

The mempool consists of the unconfirmed pool and reorg pool, and is responsible for managing unconfirmed transactions that have not yet been included in the longest proof-of-work chain. Miners usually draw verified transactions from the mempool to build up transaction [**block**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#block "A collection of transactions and associated metadata recorded as a single entity in the Tari blockchain")s.

## [**Metadata Signature**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#metadata-signature)

The metadata signature is a [**commitment and public key signature**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#commitment-and-public-key-signature), attached to a transaction output and signed with a combination of the homomorphic commitment private values (_vi_, _ki_), the spending key known only to the receiver, and the sender offset private key _kOi_ known only to the sender. This prevents malleability of the UTXO metadata.

## [**Script Signature**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#script-signature)

The script signature is an aggregated Commitment Signature (“ComSig”) signature, attached to a transaction input and signed with a combination of the homomorphic commitment private values (_vi_, _ki_), the spending key known only to the sender, and the script private key _kSi_ known only to the sender. This ensures that the script is valid and that the input data has not changed.

## [**Mimblewimble**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mimblewimble)

Mimblewimble is a privacy-centric cryptocurrency protocol. It was [**dropped**](https://download.wpsoftware.net/bitcoin/wizardry/mimblewimble.txt) in the Bitcoin Developers chatroom by an anonymous author and has since been refined by several authors, including Andrew Poelstra.

## [**Mining Server**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mining-server)

A Mining Server is responsible for constructing new blocks by bundling transactions from the [**mempool**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mempool "A memory pool for unconfirmed transactions on the base layer") of a connected [**Base Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-node "A full Tari node running on the base layer, validating and propagating Tari coin transactions
and blocks"). It also distributes Proof-of-Work tasks to Mining Workers and verifies PoW solutions.

## [**Mining Worker**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mining-worker)

A Mining Worker is responsible for performing Proof-of-Work tasks received from its parent [**Mining Server**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mining-server).

## [**Multisig**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#multisig)

Multi-signatures (Multisigs) are also known as N-of-M signatures; this means that a minimum of N number of the M peers need to agree before a transaction can be spent. N and M can be equal, which is a special case and is often referred to as an N-of-N Multisig.

[**TLU multisig**](https://tlu.tarilabs.com/cryptography/musig-schnorr-sig-scheme/The_MuSig_Schnorr_Signature_Scheme.html)

## [**Node ID**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#node-id)

A node ID is a unique identifier that specifies the location of a [**communication node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#communication-node "A communication node that is responsible for maintaining the Tari
communication network") or [**communication client**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#communication-client "A communication client that makes use of the Tari communication network,
but does not maintain it") in the Tari communication network. The node ID can either be obtained from registration on the [**Base Layer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-layer "The Tari layer handling payments and secured by proof of work") or can be derived from the public identification key of a [**communication node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#communication-node "A communication node that is responsible for maintaining the Tari
communication network") or [**communication client**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#communication-client "A communication client that makes use of the Tari communication network,
but does not maintain it").

## [**Non-fungible Token (NFT)**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#non-fungible-token-nft)

A Non-fungible token is a specific instance of a token issued as part of a [**digital asset**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset "Sets of Native digital tokens, both fungible and non-fungible that are created by
asset issuers on the Tari 2nd layer"). It is another name for a [digital asset token]. NFTs are contained within specially marked [**UTXO**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#unspent-transaction-outputs)s on the Tari Base Layer.

## [**Orphan Pool**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#orphan-pool)

The orphan pool is part of the [**mempool**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mempool "A memory pool for unconfirmed transactions on the base layer") and manages all [**transaction**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#transaction "Base layer tari coin transfers.")s that have been verified but attempt to spend [**UTXO**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#unspent-transaction-outputs)s that do not exist or haven’t been created yet.

## [**Pending Pool**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#pending-pool)

The pending pool is part of the [**mempool**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mempool "A memory pool for unconfirmed transactions on the base layer") and manages all [**transaction**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#transaction "Base layer tari coin transfers.")s that have a time-lock restriction on when it can be processed or attempt to spend [**UTXO**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#unspent-transaction-outputs)s with time-locks.

## [**Pruned Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#pruned-node)

This is a pruned history-based [**node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-node "A full Tari node running on the base layer, validating and propagating Tari coin transactions
and blocks"). It uses cryptography of [**Mimblewimble**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mimblewimble "a privacy-centric cryptocurrency protocol") to allow the removal of spent inputs and outputs beyond the **pruning horizon**.

It can still validate the integrity of the blockchain, i.e. no coins were destroyed or created beyond what is allowed by consensus rules. A sufficient number of blocks back from the tip should be configured because reorgs are no longer possible beyond that horizon.

## [**Pruning Horizon**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#pruning-horizon)

This is a local setting for each node to help reduce syncing time and bandwidth. This is the number of blocks from the chain tip beyond which a chain will be pruned.

## [**Public Nomination Mode**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#public-nomination-mode)

An asset runs in public nomination mode when the [**Asset Issuer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#asset-issuer "An entity that creates digital assets on the Tari DAN") broadcasts a call for nominations to the network, and VNs from the network nominate themselves as candidates to become members of the [**committee**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#committee "A group of validator nodes that are responsible for managing a specific Digital Asset") for the asset. The [**Asset Issuer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#asset-issuer "An entity that creates digital assets on the Tari DAN") will then employ the [**CommitteeSelectionStrategy**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#committeeselectionstrategy "A strategy for an Asset Issuer to select candidates for the
committee from the available registered Validator Nodes who responded to the nomination call for that asset") to select the committee from the list of available candidates.

## [**Range proof**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#range-proof)

A mathematical demonstration that a value inside a [**commitment**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#commitment) (i.e. it is hidden) lies within a certain range. For [**Mimblewimble**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mimblewimble "a privacy-centric cryptocurrency protocol"), range proofs are used to prove that outputs are positive values.

## [**Registration Deposit**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#registration-deposit)

An amount of Tari coin that is locked up on the base layer when a [**Validator Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#validator-node "A second-layer node that manages and validates digital asset state transitions") is registered. In order to make Sybil attacks expensive and to provide an authoritative base layer registry of [**validator node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#validator-node "A second-layer node that manages and validates digital asset state transitions")s, they will need to lock up an amount of [**Tari Coin**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#tari-coin "The base layer token") on the [**Base Layer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-layer "The Tari layer handling payments and secured by proof of work") using a registration transaction to begin acting as a VN on the DAN.

## [**Registration Term**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#registration-term)

The minimum amount of time that a VN registration lasts; the [**Registration Deposit**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#registration-deposit "An amount of tari coin that is locked up on the base layer when a
[Validator Node] is registered") can only be released after this minimum period has elapsed.

## [**Reorg Pool**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#reorg-pool)

The reorg pool is part of the [**mempool**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mempool "A memory pool for unconfirmed transactions on the base layer") and stores all [**transaction**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#transaction "Base layer tari coin transfers.")s that have recently been included in blocks in case a blockchain reorganization occurs, and the transactions need to be restored to the [**transaction pool**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#transaction-pool "A pool in the Mempool for valid and verified unconfirmed transactions").

## [**Script Keypair**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#script-keypair)

The script private - public keypair (_kSi_, _KSi_) is used in [**TariScript**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#tariscript "The Tari scripting system for transactions") to unlock and execute the script associated with an output. Afterwards, the execution stack must contain exactly one value that must be equal to the script public key.

## [**Script Offset**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#script-offset)

The script offset provides proof that every script public key _KSi_ and sender offset public key _KOi_ provided for a transaction’s inputs and outputs are correct.

## [**Sender Offset Keypair**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#sender-offset-keypair)

The sender offset private - public keypair (_kOi_, _KOi_) is used by the sender of an output to lock all its metadata by virtue of a [**metadata signature**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#metadata-signature).

## [**Spending Key**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#spending-key)

A private spending key is a private key that permits spending of a [**UTXO**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#unspent-transaction-outputs). It is also sometimes referred to as a Blinding Factor, since in Tari (and Mimblewimble) outputs, the value of a UTXO is _blinded_ by the spending key:

_C_=_v_._H_\+_k_._G_

The public key, _P_=_k_._G,_ is known as the _public_ spending key.

## [**SynchronisationState**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#synchronisationstate)

The current synchronization state of a [**Base Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-node "A full Tari node running on the base layer, validating and propagating Tari coin transactions
and blocks"). This can either be

- `starting` - The node has freshly started up and is still waiting for the first round of chain_metadata responses from its neighbours on which to base its next state change.
- `header_sync` - The node is in the process of synchronizing headers with chosen sync peer.
- `horizon_sync` - The node is in the process of syncing blocks from the tip to its [pruning horizon]
- `block_sync` - The node is in the process of syncing all blocks back to the genesis block
- `listening` - The node has completed its syncing strategy and will continue to listen for new blocks and monitor its neighbours to detect if it falls behind.

## [**SynchronisationStrategy**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#synchronisationstrategy)

The generalized approach for a [**Base Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-node "A full Tari node running on the base layer, validating and propagating Tari coin transactions
and blocks") to obtain the current state of the blockchain from the peer-to-peer network. Specific implementations may differ based on different trade-offs and constraints with respect to bandwidth, local network conditions, etc.

## [**Tari Coin**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#tari-coin)

The base layer token. Tari coins are released according to the [**emission schedule**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#emission-schedule) on the Tari [**base layer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-layer "The Tari layer handling payments and secured by proof of work") [**blockchain**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#blockchain "The linked sequence of Tari blocks on the Tari base layer") in [**coinbase transaction**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#coinbase-transaction)s.

## [**TariScript**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#tariscript)

Tari uses a scripting system for transactions, not unlike [**Bitcoin’s scripting system**](https://en.bitcoin.it/wiki/Script), called TariScript. It is also simple, stack-based, processed from left to right, not Turing-complete, with no loops. It is a list of instructions linked in a non-malleable way to each output, specifying its conditions of spending.

## [**Total Accumulated difficulty**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#total-accumulated-difficulty)

The Accumulated difficulty of the chain is used to compare chain tips. Every block has a total accumulated difficulty that is calculated as the sum of all achieved difficulties of Sha3 blocks multiplied by he sum of all achieved difficulties of RandomX blocks. This is represented as a u128.

## [**Transaction**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#transaction)

Transactions are activities recorded on the Tari [**blockchain**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#blockchain "The linked sequence of Tari blocks on the Tari base layer") running on the [**base layer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-layer "The Tari layer handling payments and secured by proof of work"). Transactions always involve a transfer of [**Tari coin**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#tari-coin "The base layer token")s. A [**mimblewimble**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mimblewimble) transaction body consists of one or more blinded inputs and outputs.

## [**Transaction Pool**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#transaction-pool)

The transaction pool is part of the [**mempool**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#mempool "A memory pool for unconfirmed transactions on the base layer") and manages all [**transaction**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#transaction "Base layer tari coin transfers.")s that have been verified, spend valid [**UTXO**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#unspent-transaction-outputs)s and don’t have any time-lock restrictions.

## [**Trusted Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#trusted-node)

A permissioned Validator Node nominated by an Asset Issuer that will form part of the committee for that Digital Asset.

## [**Token Wallet**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#token-wallet)

A Tari Token Wallet is responsible for managing **[Digital ](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset "Sets of Native digital tokens, both fungible and non-fungible that are created by
asset issuers on the Tari 2nd layer")Asset**s and [**Tokens**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset-tokens "or just, "tokens". The tokens associated with a given digital asset. Tokens are created
by asset issuers"), and for constructing and negotiating [**instruction**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#instructions "Second-layer network commands for managing digital asset state")s for transferring and receiving Assets and Tokens on the [**Digital Asset Network**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset-network "The Tari second layer. All digital asset interactions are managed here.").

## [**Transaction Weight**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#transaction-weight)

The weight of a transaction/block is measured in “grams”. See [**Block / Transaction weight**](https://rfc.tari.com/RFC-0110_BaseNodes.html#blocktransaction-weight) for more details.

## [**Unspent transaction outputs**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#unspent-transaction-outputs)

An unspent transaction output (UTXO) is a discrete number of Tari that are available to be spent. The sum of all UTXOs represents all the Tari currently in circulation. In addition, the sum of all UTXO values equals the sum of the [**block reward**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#block-reward "The amount of Tari created in every block")s for all blocks up to the current block height.

UTXO values are hidden by their [**commitment**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#commitment)s. Only the owner of the UTXO and (presumably) the creator of the UTXO (either a [**Coinbase transaction**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#coinbase-transaction) or the previous spender) know the value of the UTXO.

## [**Transaction Kernel**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#transaction-kernel)

A piece of data that is always kept as part of the blockchain and never pruned away. This contains the excess signature and serves as proof that the parties transacting know the blinding factors of their [**commitment**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#commitment)s used in the transaction.

## [**Validator Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#validator-node)

Validator nodes (VNs) make up the Tari second layer, or [**Digital Asset Network**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset-network "The Tari second layer. All digital asset interactions are managed here."). VNs are responsible for creating and updating [**digital asset**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#digital-asset "Sets of Native digital tokens, both fungible and non-fungible that are created by
asset issuers on the Tari 2nd layer")s living on the Tari network.

## [**Validator Node committee**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#validator-node-committee)

Validator nodes (VNs) validate and execute instructions on the Digital Assets Network (DAN). They are also responsible for maintaining state in DAN assets.

## [**Wallet**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#wallet)

A Tari Wallet is responsible for managing key pairs and for constructing and negotiating [**transaction**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#transaction "Base layer tari coin transfers.")s for transferring and receiving [**tari coin**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#tari-coin "The base layer token")s on the [**Base Layer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-layer "The Tari layer handling payments and secured by proof of work").
