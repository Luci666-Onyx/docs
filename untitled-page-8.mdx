---
title: "Tari Network Terminology"
---

Below is a list of terms and their definitions used throughout the Tari code and documentation. Let’s use this glossary to disambiguate ideas and work towards a [**ubiquitous language**](https://blog.carbonfive.com/2016/10/04/ubiquitous-language-the-joy-of-naming/) for this project.

## [**Archive node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#archive-node)

This is a full history [**<u>base node</u>**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-node "A full Tari node running on the base layer, validating and propagating Tari coin transactions
and blocks"). It will keep a complete history of every transaction ever received, and it will not implement pruning.

## [**Asset Collateral**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#assetcollateral)

The amount of tari coin a [**Validator Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#validator-node "A second-layer node that manages and validates digital asset state transitions") must stake on the [**base layer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-layer "The Tari layer handling payments and secured by proof of work") to become part of an asset [**committee**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#committee "A group of validator nodes that are responsible for managing a specific Digital Asset").

## [**Asset Issuer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#asset-issuer)

An entity that creates digital assets on the Tari DAN. The Asset Issuer will specify the parameters of the contract template that defines the rules that govern the asset and the number and nature of its constituent tokens on issuance. The Asset Issuer will, generally, be the initial owner of the tokens.

## [**Bad Actor**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#bad-actor)

A participant that acts maliciously or negligently to the detriment of the network or another participant.

## [**Base layer**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-layer)

The Tari Base layer is a merge-mined [**blockchain**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#blockchain "The linked sequence of Tari blocks on the Tari base layer") secured by proof-of-work. The base layer is primarily responsible for the emission of new Tari and for securing and managing [**Tari coin**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#tari-coin "The base layer token") transfers.

## [**Base Node**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#base-node)

A full Tari node running on the base layer. Its primary role is validating and propagating [**Tari coin**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#tari-coin "The base layer token") transactions and blocks to the rest of the network.

## [**Block**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#block)

A collection of transactions and associated metadata recorded as a single entity in the Tari blockchain. The ordering of Tari transactions is set purely by the block height of the block they are recorded in.

## [**Block Header**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#block-header)

A data structure that validates the information contained in a [**block**](https://rfc.tari.com/Glossary#block "A collection of transactions and associated metadata recorded as a single entity in the Tari blockchain").
