Tari Universe is the beautifully simple app for mining Tari on Mac or PC. Tari Universe turns your computer into a money machine. It works by harnessing your computers computational power to solve blocks on the Tari network. In doing so, you're helping to secure the Tari network while earning Tari token (XTM) rewards.
