Tari is a community-driven project. This repository contains the Tari Improvement Proposals, which are all technical documents, community standards and governance practices.

## **Tari Improvement Proposals (TIP)**

Tari Improvement Proposals are proposals for improving the Tari project generally. Ideas and questions are posted in the [**Tari Discord**](https://discord.gg/hPABK5WV). This is typically short-form content with rapid feedback. Often, these conversations will lead to someone posting an [**issue**](https://github.com/tari-project/tari/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3ARFC "Tari RFC Issues") or TIP [**pull request**](https://github.com/tari-project/tari/pulls?q=is%3Aopen+is%3Apr+label%3ARFC "Tari RFC pull requests"). New TIPs should be posted to the [**community forums**](https://community.tari.com/ "Tari Community Forums") for visibility and community review, and should follow the format given in the [**TIP template**](https://app.mintlify.com/tari-d217ee87/tari-d217ee87/~/24882750-1221-404a-bea0-fd669d3d9716).

The format and standards of Tari Improvement Proposals will be covered in [**TIP-1**](https://rfc.tari.com/TIP-0001_tari_improvement_proposals.html "TIP-1: Tari Improvement Proposals").
