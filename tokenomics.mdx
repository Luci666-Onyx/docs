Tari's economic design reflects a core belief that a healthy blockchain needs long-term miner incentives, fair distribution to early contributors, and a credible link between its two network layers. This page explains the two tokens in the Tari ecosystem, how XTM is emitted over time, how the 30% pre-mine is structured and vested, and how the Turbine Model connects Minotari's base layer to the Ootle's smart contract economy.

## Two Tokens: XTM and TARI

Tari's two-layer architecture is reflected in its two-token model.

**XTM** is the native token of **Minotari**, Tari's Layer 1 proof-of-work blockchain. XTM is earned by miners, used to pay Layer 1 transaction fees, and serves as the economic anchor for the entire protocol.

**TARI** is the native token of **the Ootle**, Tari's Layer 2 smart contract network. TARI can only be created by burning XTM on Layer 1 at a 1:1 ratio — there is no independent emission of TARI. Every TARI token in existence represents XTM that was permanently removed from the Minotari supply.

## Total Supply and Emission Schedule

The total supply of XTM is **21 billion tokens**. The full supply is emitted over approximately **27.8 years** via an exponential decay function. Unlike Bitcoin's discrete four-year halving events, Tari's block reward declines smoothly **block by block**, with the reward approximately halving roughly every three years.

Once annual emissions fall to **1% of the total XTM supply**, the protocol transitions to a **perpetual 1% annual tail emission**. This tail emission continues indefinitely, ensuring miners always have a baseline economic incentive to secure Minotari — even long after the initial supply curve has flattened.

<Tip>
  The tail emission is not a bug or a concession — it is an intentional design decision. A blockchain without miner incentives is a blockchain with weakening security over time. Tari's 1% tail emission keeps the long-term security model sustainable.
</Tip>

## Mining Rewards

Miners receive **70% of the initial XTM emission**. This breaks down as follows:

- **100% of all block rewards** flow directly to miners.
- **100% of Layer 1 transaction fees** are paid to miners.
- Block rewards are distributed equally across Minotari's four mining algorithms — each algorithm (RxM, RxT, SHA3x, and Cuckaroo) receives **25% of every block reward**.

The remaining 30% of the initial emission is allocated to the pre-mine, described below.

## Pre-Mine Allocation (30%)

To fund the people and organisations who built Tari and to seed community growth, 30% of the total XTM supply is reserved as a pre-mine. All pre-mine allocations are subject to vesting schedules — none of it is liquid at launch except for a specific liquidity provision described below.

| Allocation | % of Supply | Vesting Schedule |
|---|---|---|
| **Community** | 5% | Unlocks monthly over 12 months, starting 6 months after mainnet launch |
| **Protocol Infrastructure & Grants** | 9% | 40% available at launch for liquidity provision; remainder unlocks monthly over 4 years starting 6 months after mainnet |
| **Contributors** | 4% | Unlocks monthly over 5 years, starting 12 months after mainnet launch |
| **Participants (early supporters)** | 12% | Unlocks monthly over 2 years, starting 12 months after mainnet launch |
| **Total Pre-Mine** | **30%** | — |

### Community (5%)

The community allocation funds incentive programs, grants, and ecosystem development initiatives. Tokens from this tranche begin unlocking monthly **six months after mainnet** and are fully distributed over the following 12 months.

### Protocol Infrastructure & Grants (9%)

This allocation supports long-term protocol development, infrastructure, and ecosystem grants. **40% of this tranche is available at launch** to provide liquidity on exchanges and decentralised markets. The remaining 60% unlocks monthly over **four years**, beginning six months after mainnet.

### Contributors (4%)

Contributors are the developers, researchers, and team members who built the Tari protocol. Their allocation begins unlocking monthly **12 months after mainnet** and is fully vested over the following five years.

### Participants (12%)

Participants are early supporters who backed Tari in its pre-launch phase. This is the largest pre-mine tranche. Tokens begin unlocking monthly **12 months after mainnet** and vest fully over two years.

## The Turbine Model and TARI Economics

The economic connection between XTM and TARI is managed by the **Turbine Model**, which governs how TARI is created and destroyed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Burn XTM to Mint TARI">
    To obtain TARI for use on the Ootle, you burn XTM on Minotari at a **1:1 ratio**. The XTM is permanently destroyed and the equivalent amount of TARI is minted on the Ootle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use TARI for Ootle Fees">
    All transaction fees on the Ootle are paid in TARI. This creates organic demand for TARI proportional to usage of the smart contract layer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Burn a Portion of Fees">
    A portion of every Ootle transaction fee is **burned**, permanently removing TARI from circulation. This introduces a deflationary pressure on TARI that is directly tied to network activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Throttle Algorithm Adjusts the Burn Rate">
    The **Throttle** algorithm continuously monitors the state of both layers and dynamically adjusts how much of each fee is burned versus retained. This mechanism is designed to maintain the **soft 1:1 peg** between XTM and TARI and to stabilise the broader ecosystem over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Turbine Model creates a feedback loop between the two layers: as Ootle usage grows, more TARI is burned, which increases demand for burning XTM to create new TARI, which in turn benefits XTM holders and miners on Minotari.
