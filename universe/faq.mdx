Whether you are brand new to Tari or have been mining for a while, you probably have questions. Below you will find answers to the most common ones. If something is not covered here, join the Tari community on Discord or Telegram — the community is active and welcoming to newcomers.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="What is Tari Universe?">
    Tari Universe is a free desktop application that lets your computer mine XTM — the native token of the Tari blockchain — while you go about your day. It is available for Mac, Windows, and Linux.

    When Tari Universe is running, your CPU and GPU are solving cryptographic proof-of-work puzzles that validate transactions and secure the Tari network. In return for this work, you earn XTM tokens, which are credited directly to a built-in self-custody wallet inside the app. No exchange account, no sign-up, no personal information required.

    Tari Universe also includes a real-time earnings display, an in-app rewards game (the Tari Airdrop game), and a companion mobile wallet (available on iOS and Android) so you can track your XTM balance anywhere.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the catch?">
    There is no catch. You earn real XTM tokens in exchange for your computer's computational work. This is how proof-of-work blockchains operate: miners provide the security, and the protocol compensates them with newly issued tokens.

    Tari Universe does not run hidden processes beyond mining, does not sell your data, and does not require any personal information to use. The app — and the entire Tari protocol — is fully open source. Anyone can inspect every line of code on [GitHub](https://github.com/tari-project/tari).

    The only cost to you is electricity and the use of your CPU and GPU cycles while the app is running. You can pause mining at any time with a single click.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is this safe?">
    Yes. Tari Universe is safe to run on your personal computer. Here is why:

    - **Open source** — Every line of code in Tari Universe and the Tari protocol is publicly available on GitHub. Independent security researchers can and do review it.
    - **No personal data required** — You do not create an account or provide any personal information. Your wallet is identified only by a cryptographic key pair that is generated locally on your device.
    - **Standard mining activity** — Tari Universe uses your CPU and GPU to perform mathematical computations, the same activity used by all proof-of-work cryptocurrencies. It does not access your files, camera, microphone, or network activity beyond what is needed to connect to the Tari mining pool.
    - **Self-custody wallet** — Your XTM is stored in a wallet on your device, not on a third-party server. Only you hold the keys.

    As with any software, you should always download Tari Universe from the official source: [tari.com/downloads](https://tari.com/downloads) or the official Mac App Store listing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the minimum system requirements to mine Tari?">
    To run Tari Universe comfortably, your machine should meet the following minimums:

    | Component | Minimum Requirement |
    |---|---|
    | **Operating System** | macOS 10.15 (Catalina)+, Windows 10+, Ubuntu 20.04+ |
    | **RAM** | 4 GB (8 GB or more recommended) |
    | **Storage** | 1 GB free space (SSD strongly preferred) |
    | **CPU** | 64-bit multi-core processor |
    | **Internet** | Stable broadband connection |

    A dedicated GPU is optional but will meaningfully increase your mining rewards through the SHA3x algorithm. Tari Universe detects your hardware and adjusts automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will this slow down my computer?">
    Tari Universe is designed to use your computer's **spare** computational resources — the CPU and GPU cycles that would otherwise sit idle. In practice:

    - Light everyday tasks (browsing, email, documents, video calls) are generally not noticeably affected.
    - If you start a resource-intensive task like gaming, video rendering, or compiling code, you may notice some competition for CPU or GPU time.
    - You can **pause mining at any time** with a single click in the app, and resume it just as easily when you are done.

    Tari Universe also does not run at startup by default, so it only consumes resources when you have it open and mining is active.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to understand cryptocurrency to use this?">
    Not at all. Tari Universe is designed to be the most accessible mining application ever built. You do not need to understand blockchain technology, proof-of-work algorithms, wallets, seed phrases, or any other cryptocurrency concept to get started.

    The app walks you through everything step by step: it creates your wallet automatically, starts mining with sensible defaults, and displays your earnings in plain language. If you ever want to go deeper, the documentation and community are here to help — but understanding the technology is never a prerequisite for using it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What makes Tari different from other crypto projects?">
    Tari has several properties that set it apart:

    - **Default confidential** — Tari is built on the Mimblewimble protocol, which means transaction amounts and participant identities are private by default. Unlike Bitcoin, where all transactions are visible on a public ledger, Tari keeps your financial activity private without any extra steps.
    - **Proof of work** — Tari uses proof-of-work mining (not proof-of-stake), making it accessible to anyone with a computer and resistant to centralisation by wealthy token holders.
    - **Four mining lanes** — Tari's unique multi-algorithm approach means CPU miners, GPU miners, and merge-miners from the Monero ecosystem can all participate simultaneously, spreading rewards across a wider community and improving network security.
    - **Built for real people** — Tari Universe is genuinely designed to be beautiful and easy to use, not just functional. The goal is to bring the benefits of decentralised finance to people who have never touched a cryptocurrency before.
    - **Open source** — Every component of the Tari protocol and its applications is publicly available for inspection, modification, and contribution.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the network warm-up period?">
    When the Tari mainnet first launched, the network entered a **warm-up period** — an early phase during which the mining difficulty algorithm is still calibrating to the actual number of miners and hash rate on the network.

    During the warm-up period, you may notice that your rewards are lower or less consistent than they will be once the network has stabilised. This is completely normal and expected. The Tari difficulty adjustment algorithm is designed to gradually converge on a stable block time of approximately one minute.

    The warm-up period is temporary. As the network grows and difficulty settles, block rewards become more predictable and consistent for all miners.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I win rewards (gems, hammers, etc.)?">
    Tari Universe includes a built-in **Tari Airdrop game** that lets you earn bonus XTM rewards and in-app items on top of your regular mining income:

    - **Gems** — Earned by inviting friends to mine using your personal referral link. Each friend who installs Tari Universe and starts mining earns you gems. Gems can be exchanged for additional XTM rewards.
    - **Hammers** — A special resource that amplifies your gem earnings. Hammers are collected through consistent daily participation, completing in-app challenges, and community events. The more you mine and engage, the more hammers you accumulate.

    To find your referral link, open the **Airdrop** or **Rewards** section inside Tari Universe. Share that link with friends, on social media, or in communities where people might be interested in mining.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I had a testnet wallet — can I use that seed phrase for mainnet?">
    No. Your old testnet seed phrase **cannot** be used for mainnet Tari Universe, and you should not attempt to import it.

    The testnet was a separate test network used for experimenting with features before mainnet launched. Testnet XTM had no real-world value and the testnet blockchain operated independently from the mainnet blockchain. The two networks use different parameters and are cryptographically distinct.

    When you install Tari Universe for mainnet, the app will automatically generate a fresh wallet with a new seed phrase. Make sure to back up your new seed phrase immediately — it is the only way to recover your mainnet XTM.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the Testnet?">
    The **Testnet** is a separate Tari network that runs in parallel with the main Tari network (Mainnet). It is designed for experimentation and development — a safe environment where new features, protocol changes, and applications can be tested without risking real funds.

    Testnet XTM tokens have **no monetary value**. They exist purely for testing purposes and are not transferable to or from the mainnet. If you mine on the testnet, the tokens you earn are test tokens only.

    Developers and technically curious users sometimes run testnet nodes to help stress-test new releases or explore the protocol before changes go live on mainnet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why should I mine on Testnet?">
    If you want to explore Tari's features without any risk, the testnet is a great environment for it. On testnet you can:

    - Experiment with sending and receiving XTM transactions without using real funds
    - Try out new features that have not yet been released to mainnet
    - Help the Tari development team by stress-testing new builds and reporting bugs
    - Get comfortable with how the wallet and mining UI work before switching to mainnet

    Because testnet tokens have no value, there is nothing to lose. It is the ideal sandbox for learning how everything works.
  </Accordion>
