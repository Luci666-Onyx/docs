Tari Universe is a beautifully designed desktop application that turns your computer into an XTM miner. When it runs, your CPU and GPU solve proof-of-work blocks on the Tari network — and you earn real XTM tokens in return. Over 700,000 miners around the world are already using it, and getting started takes just a few minutes with no cryptocurrency knowledge required.

## What Is Tari Universe?

Tari Universe is the official desktop mining application for the Tari blockchain. It harnesses your computer's processing power to secure the Tari network through proof-of-work mining, rewarding you with Tari tokens (XTM) for every block your machine helps solve. The app handles all the technical complexity behind the scenes — no command-line tools, no configuration files, no prior mining experience needed.

Beyond mining, Tari Universe includes a built-in wallet so your earnings are held securely on your own device from the moment you start. A companion mobile wallet (Tari Universe Wallet) for iOS and Android lets you track your balance and send or receive XTM from anywhere.

## How Mining Works

When Tari Universe is open and mining is active, your computer continuously competes to solve cryptographic puzzles that validate batches of transactions on the Tari network. Each time a block is solved, the miners who contributed computational power share the XTM block reward. Your earnings accumulate directly in your built-in wallet in real time.

Tari supports two mining algorithms — **RandomX** (CPU-optimised) and **SHA3x** (CPU/GPU) — as well as merge-mining with Monero. Tari Universe selects and manages these automatically so you don't need to think about it.

## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Tari Universe">
    Visit [tari.com/downloads](https://tari.com/downloads) and download the installer for your operating system. Mac users can also find it on the Mac App Store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Run the installer and follow the on-screen prompts. On macOS, drag the app to your Applications folder. On Windows, run the `.exe` installer and click through the setup wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your wallet">
    When Tari Universe opens for the first time, it will generate a new XTM wallet for you automatically. Write down your 24-word seed phrase when prompted — this is the only way to recover your funds if you lose access to your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start mining">
    Click **Start Mining**. Tari Universe will sync with the network and begin mining. You will see your hashrate, estimated earnings, and recent blocks in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track your earnings">
    Your XTM balance updates as blocks are solved. You can pause mining at any time using the toggle in the app, or set the app to use only spare resources so it doesn't interfere with your other work.
  </Step>
</Steps>
